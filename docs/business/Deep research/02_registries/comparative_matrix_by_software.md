## Watchlist

- Bentley SYNCHRO, Planisware, Safran Planner, and Smartsheet remain in the watchlist because their open official documentation is less immediately inspectable or less field-schema-explicit in the current pass.
- They may still be strategically important, but the current research corpus prioritizes software with stronger open evidence for entities, fields, codings, and traceability behavior.
