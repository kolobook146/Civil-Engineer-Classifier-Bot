## Main Stage 23 Reading

- `PMIS` is the broadest subsystem and often contains links to all the others.
- `EDMS / CDE` is the most explicit project-record system.
- `Permit / authority` is the most externally governed subsystem.
- `Smart-site` is the richest event and telemetry subsystem.
- `Resource optimization` is the clearest capacity-balancing subsystem.
- `Payment / commercial` is the strongest legal-financial control subsystem.
