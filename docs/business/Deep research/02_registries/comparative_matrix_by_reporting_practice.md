# Comparative Matrix by Reporting Practice

This matrix compares how major construction-investment organizations structure reporting and dashboard practice across the five curator zones used in the research corpus.

| Region | Primary reporting container | Main cadence | Core KPI families | Dominant system environment | Strengths | Weaknesses / blind spots | Implications for pilot dashboard design |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `USA` | Monthly controls pack plus owner dashboard; PMIS-backed status reporting | Monthly, with weekly exceptions and formal variance review | Baseline vs forecast vs actual milestones, EV and variance, cost, risk, corrective action, funding, project health | Owner handbook + PMIS + EV / project dashboard environment | Strongest explicit integrated controls grammar; clear RAG logic; monthly variance management is formalized | Can become governance-heavy and assurance-heavy; public external reporting is less useful as a PMO template | Keep EVA, RAG, variance narrative, and monthly controls structure; avoid importing full federal assurance bureaucracy into the pilot |
| `EU` | Programme handbook plus periodic reporting pack plus handover / transition appendices | Weekly and period-end reporting, plus stage-gate and handover reviews | Milestones, one-page programmes, schedule narrative, handover readiness, interfaces, risk, programme governance | Programme controls office + handbook-led planning + transition governance | Strongest stage-gate, programme integration, and handover logic | Public corporate reporting is financially strong but less explicit on PMO mechanics than owner handbooks | Strengthen phase, gate, milestone, readiness, and handover sections in the pilot reporting layer |
| `CIS` | Monitoring-oriented digital-control layer plus administrative and delivery-cycle reporting | Operational monitoring, administrative reporting cycles, and management reviews | Project-cycle duration, object progress, issue control, document and registry state, digital monitoring, portfolio scale | Digital vertical, state information systems, smart-site supervision, large-programme enterprise reporting | Strongest administrative visibility and cycle-monitoring orientation; good fit for portfolio monitoring and delivery transparency | Open-source corpus is weaker on one formal monthly controls-pack grammar; investor reporting is often broader than PMO reporting | Add cycle, permit, and registry visibility; keep a compact controls pack rather than trying to mimic a full U.S.-style PMO handbook |
| `China` | Smart-site cockpit and digital platform dashboard with management briefing layer | Near-real-time operational monitoring plus periodic business briefings | Progress, quality, safety, digital events, contract flow, project health, management alerts | Platformized digital-governance environment with smart-site and cockpit tools | Strongest cockpit and platform-native reporting logic; high operational visibility | Open enterprise corpus is less explicit on narrative controls packs and formal exception memoranda than USA / Middle East | Add cockpit-like operational cards, issue visibility, and system-linked progress views; do not collapse reporting to finance-only investor metrics |
| `Middle East` | Owner-mandated PMIS plus cost-loaded master schedule reporting plus PMO / TPMO review pack | Monthly formal controls cycle with milestone and commercial linkage; weekly escalation where needed | Cost-loaded master schedule, baseline vs estimated vs actual milestones, change, payment, KPI, EDMS, handover, commissioning | PMIS / PMDS / Primavera / owner controls environment | Strongest owner-mandated PMIS and reporting discipline in the open corpus; schedule is tightly tied to payment, change, and document governance | Can become megaproject-heavy and over-specified for a pilot product | Borrow the PMIS discipline, milestone governance, and exception logic; defer full payment / claims / EDMS workflow integration |
