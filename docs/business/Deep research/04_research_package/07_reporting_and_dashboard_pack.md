## Compact Comparison

| Region | Reporting signature | Strongest takeaway |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `USA` | Integrated controls dashboard plus monthly variance discipline | Best source for EV, RAG, and corrective-action governance |
| `EU` | Programme handbook plus periodic pack plus handover governance | Best source for phase, gate, interface, and readiness reporting |
| `CIS` | Monitoring-led digital visibility plus cycle and registry management | Best source for delivery-cycle and blocker transparency |
| `China` | Smart-site cockpit and platform-native reporting | Best source for operational dashboard patterns and platform-linked signals |
| `Middle East` | Owner-mandated PMIS plus cost-loaded master schedule and PMO review | Best source for PMIS discipline, milestone governance, and commercial linkage |

## Recommended Reporting Stack for Our Product

### 1. Executive dashboard

- portfolio status by project and phase
- baseline versus current delta
- `Earned Value Analysis (EVA)` or equivalent cost-schedule indicators
- top risks and upcoming gates

### 2. Monthly controls pack

- project summary
- schedule and milestone status
- cost and commercial status
- risk and change
- procurement and permits
- `Health, Safety, and Environment (HSE)`, quality, handover, and readiness

### 3. Project drill-down sheet

- baseline versus current task-level view
- phase and package drill-down
- critical-path and near-term milestone pressure

### 4. Exception register layer

- top blockers
- permit and procurement exceptions
- change and risk registers
- late handover and readiness issues

## Do Not Import Yet

The following patterns are real in enterprise environments but too heavy for the current pilot:

- fully formal PMO / TPMO committee pack mechanics
- dense document approval workflows inside the dashboard layer
- full payment, claims, and contract-intelligence subsystem
- mature external investor-reporting templates as a product requirement

## Result

Stage 24 strengthens the earlier enterprise-practice findings by showing that the reporting model should be layered:

- a compact executive surface;
- a governed controls-pack layer;
- a drill-down layer;
- an exception layer.
