# Stage 13-15 Synthesis: Software and Data Model Alignment

## Purpose

This synthesis connects the Stage 13-15 findings with the earlier regional and institute base. The main question is not "what software features exist?" but "how does software evidence confirm, reshape, or challenge the model emerging from regions and project institutes?"

## Main Conclusion

Software documentation does not contradict the regional and institute base. It sharpens it.

The combined base now strongly supports five families:

1. `Decomposition`
2. `Document-system`
3. `Stage-gate`
4. `Report/log`
5. `Digital-governance`

Earlier stages discovered these families indirectly through regions and institutes. Stage 13-15 confirms that real software products implement them in different combinations.

## Alignment with the Four Regions

### CIS

Earlier regional finding:

- scheduling is embedded in organizational-technological documentation;
- `POS`, `PPR`, and related planning artefacts are central.

Software alignment:

- `Primavera Unifier` confirms governed schedule sheets and master/child sheet structures.
- `Asta Powerproject` confirms document-adjacent and code-library-rich planning containers.

Result:

The CIS profile is strengthened. A document-system schedule grammar is not a regional anomaly; it has software analogues.

### EU

Earlier regional finding:

- the strongest common EU layer is project-cycle, procurement, stage-gate, and public-delivery governance.

Software alignment:

- `Primavera Cloud`, `Zoho`, and `8Manage` confirm phase-aware, configurable, governance-linked planning environments.
- software does not erase the stage-gate layer; it gives it configurable and digital forms.

Result:

The EU profile remains governance- and phase-led, and software evidence supports rather than weakens that interpretation.

### USA

Earlier regional finding:

- the U.S. has the strongest explicit schedule quality, review, baseline, and integrated master schedule culture.

Software alignment:

- `Primavera P6`, `Primavera Cloud`, `Microsoft Project`, and `Deltek Open Plan` strongly reinforce decomposition, coding, baseline, and control-account logic.
- `Autodesk Build` and `Procore` extend this with collaborative field layers rather than replacing classical CPM controls.

Result:

The U.S. profile becomes even more coherent: it is the strongest combined decomposition-plus-governance-plus-quality-assurance school in the corpus.

### China

Earlier regional finding:

- China is dual-track: classic construction organization and network planning plus digital supervision and intelligent-construction growth.

Software alignment:

- `Glodon` strongly reinforces the digital-governance and linked-object layer.
- `TAPD` reinforces open-platform and evolving digital workflow logic.
- `8Manage` contributes PMO and governance signals.

Result:

The China profile is strongly confirmed. Software evidence does not collapse China into generic CPM; it validates the digital platform direction.

## Alignment with Global Project Institutes

### PMI

Most aligned software signals:

- Primavera P6
- Primavera Cloud
- Microsoft Project
- Zoho

Interpretation:

PMI's decomposition-led framing maps cleanly to software with strong WBS, task, and configurable-field grammars.

### AACE

Most aligned software signals:

- Primavera P6
- Primavera Cloud
- Deltek Open Plan

Interpretation:

AACE's controls-oriented logic is best reflected in software that exposes baselines, statusing, control mapping, and enterprise schedule structure.

### PM²

Most aligned software signals:

- Primavera Unifier
- Primavera Cloud
- Zoho
- 8Manage

Interpretation:

PM²'s artefact and phase orientation is easier to align with configurable, governance-linked environments than with pure activity engines alone.

### FIDIC and EIB/EPEC

Most aligned software signals:

- Primavera Cloud
- 8Manage
- Zoho

Interpretation:

Contract and public-delivery governance require phase, package, milestone, and approval-capable models. These are present in configurable platforms, not only in CPM engines.

### GAO and U.S. owner-control systems

Most aligned software signals:

- Primavera P6
- Deltek Open Plan
- Primavera Cloud

Interpretation:

Integrated master schedule and quality-review logic still maps most cleanly to enterprise controls software.

## What Changed Relative to the Earlier Base

### Strongly confirmed

- WBS and decomposition are universal but not sufficient.
- schedule governance depends on more than activities and dates.
- linked reports, logs, and auditability are real model elements.
- digital references and linked objects are increasingly central.

### Newly sharpened

- governed containers such as schedule sheets and templates are real schedule objects;
- version history is distinct from baseline in several tools;
- collaborative production metrics such as `Percent Plan Complete (PPC)` and root-cause logging are not fringe ideas;
- issue-centric and platform-centric planning models must be kept in scope.

### Still uneven

- Asia software field-schema visibility is less even than U.S./UK enterprise schedule documentation;
- some important tools remain watchlist products because open documentation is thinner than research needs.

## Practical Outcome for Later Stages

Stages 13-15 materially improve the research base for later work:

- Stage 16 can now compare academic teaching not just to schedule theory, but also to software-native data structures.
- Stage 17 can now test corporate practice against concrete software object families.
- Stage 21 will be able to design a target model from a wider and better evidenced universe, rather than from a task-only assumption set.

## Final Synthesis Statement

The main result of Stage 13-15 is not a list of software features. It is a stronger business-model hypothesis:

real construction and project scheduling lives simultaneously as a work network, a governed document system, a stage-gate delivery model, a report/log environment, and a digital-governance layer.
