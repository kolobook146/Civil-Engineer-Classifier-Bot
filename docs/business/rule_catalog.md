# Business Rule Catalog

Status: normative Stage 22 rule set.

## Rules

| Rule ID | Name | Applies To | Rule | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `BR-001` | Two-register model | Schedule, facts | `schedule` and `data_facts` are separate business registers. | The schedule is a governed plan/control model; facts are operational evidence. |
| `BR-002` | Hybrid schedule scope | Schedule | The schedule must support both production and project-delivery logic. | The project is broader than site execution only. |
| `BR-003` | Schedule item is the core object | Schedule | The primary schedule business object is `ScheduleItem`, not a flat task-only row concept. | Stage 22 formalizes one unified work/governance item model. |
| `BR-004` | Function-stage coordinate is mandatory in the pilot | Schedule, facts | Every pilot schedule row and every persisted fact must have `function + stage`. | This remains the minimum semantic coordinate of the current pilot. |
| `BR-005` | Function is a workstream | Schedule, facts | `function` describes the business workstream, not a task status or physical trade only. | Preserves non-construction delivery streams. |
| `BR-006` | Stage is a lifecycle position | Schedule, facts | `stage` describes lifecycle position, not generic completion status. | Prevents semantic confusion between process stage and progress. |
| `BR-007` | Work type is secondary | Schedule, facts | `work_type` refines physical scope but does not replace `function` or `stage`. | Physical scope alone cannot represent the full project model. |
| `BR-008` | Work type may be empty | Schedule, facts | `work_type` may remain empty when the item or fact is not a physical construction scope. | Design, permit, payment, and handover items may have no physical trade. |
| `BR-009` | Common project processes are explicit | Schedule | Design, approvals, procurement, payment, control, readiness, and handover must appear explicitly in the schedule model. | Delivery governance must not be hidden in comments or side files. |
| `BR-010` | Versions are immutable after publication | Schedule | A published `ScheduleVersion` must not be overwritten in place. | Version history and auditability depend on immutability. |
| `BR-011` | Baseline is a designation on a version | Schedule | Baseline must be modeled as a formal designation attached to a schedule version. | Preserves the distinction between revision history and baseline status. |
| `BR-012` | Dependencies must be explicit | Schedule | Predecessor-successor logic must be stored explicitly rather than assumed. | Hidden sequencing is not acceptable for control and analytics. |
| `BR-013` | Hierarchy and coding are separate | Schedule | Roll-up structure and reusable classification must not be merged into one overloaded field. | Different regional and software grammars need both dimensions. |
| `BR-014` | Every schedule item has a contour | Schedule | Every schedule item must declare `production`, `delivery`, or `cross-project` contour. | Production and delivery universes must remain distinguishable. |
| `BR-015` | Delivery items require lifecycle classification | Schedule | Delivery-oriented items must carry `stage` or equivalent lifecycle classification. | Permits, approvals, procurement, and handover need explicit lifecycle logic. |
| `BR-016` | Detailed production items need package or location context | Schedule | Detailed production items should carry at least `package`, `location/system`, or both. | Physical execution control requires more than WBS only. |
| `BR-017` | Governance records are first-class | Schedule | Approvals, issues, changes, KPI reviews, completion signals, and similar events must not be reduced to free text only. | The schedule model must represent governance reality. |
| `BR-018` | External systems are linked, not copied wholesale | Schedule | PMIS, EDMS, BIM, permit, authority, payment, and issue systems remain external and are linked through references. | Avoids turning the pilot core into a monolithic PMIS clone. |
| `BR-019` | Facts require confirmation | Facts | A fact must be confirmed before persistence to the business register. | The pilot trust model requires an explicit confirmation checkpoint. |
| `BR-020` | Raw fact text is always stored | Facts | `raw_text` must always be preserved in the fact register. | Auditability and later reinterpretation depend on source preservation. |
| `BR-021` | Dictionaries govern controlled fields | Schedule, facts | `function`, `stage`, `work_type`, and `unit` must come from controlled dictionaries. | Stable vocabulary is necessary for analytics and consistency. |
| `BR-022` | Facts are append-only evidence | Facts | A persisted fact record is evidence and must not be silently rewritten into a different event. | Evidence integrity matters more than convenience. |
| `BR-023` | Facts do not auto-mutate the schedule | Schedule, facts | A new fact must not automatically overwrite schedule structure or baseline data. | Mapping has material business risk and must remain controlled. |
| `BR-024` | Mapping follows explicit precedence | Schedule, facts | Fact-to-schedule linkage must use an explicit matching order, not ad hoc heuristics only. | Safe mapping depends on deterministic business precedence. |
| `BR-025` | Unmatched facts remain visible | Facts | If a fact cannot be safely matched, it must remain visible for review rather than being dropped. | Silent loss is worse than unresolved ambiguity. |
| `BR-026` | Actuals and progress are statused facts | Schedule, facts | Actual dates and progress updates must be stored as time-stamped facts or statused updates, not as baseline overwrites only. | Preserves comparison and auditability. |
| `BR-027` | Extensions use generic patterns first | Schedule | New requirements should first reuse hierarchy, coding, governance, external-link, and attribute patterns before introducing new subsystems. | Keeps the pilot extensible without premature overengineering. |
| `BR-028` | Heavy extensions stay bounded until proven necessary | Schedule | PMIS, EDMS, permit / authority, smart-site, resource optimization, and payment / commercial families remain external bounded systems until pilot evidence proves the current bridge patterns insufficient. | Prevents accidental drift from a bounded pilot core into an unplanned enterprise PMIS clone. |
| `BR-029` | Pilot workbook uses baseline and current surfaces | Schedule | The pilot schedule workbook must use separate `schedule_baseline` and `schedule_current` sheets with aligned row schema. | This keeps comparison explicit without collapsing baseline and current control into one mutable surface. |
| `BR-030` | Task ID is immutable and non-reusable | Schedule | `Task ID` is the single surfaced pilot identifier, uses project-banded pilot ranges such as `T1xxxx` to `T7xxxx`, must remain stable after creation, and must never be reused. New IDs must be checked against both the target sheet and `used_task_ids`. | Stable identifiers are required for dependencies, dashboard comparison, and bot lookup. |
| `BR-031` | Work type may describe physical-scope support | Schedule, facts | `work_type` may be used for direct execution rows and for delivery or support rows tied to a physical work scope. | Tendering, design, supply, and commissioning can still be organized around physical scope. |
| `BR-032` | Successor references are derived | Schedule | Users must maintain only `Predecessor Task IDs`; `Successor Task IDs` must be system-derived. | One-directional dependency entry reduces inconsistency and editing overhead. |
| `BR-033` | Planned and actual quantity-cost are separated | Schedule | The pilot sheet must store `Planned Quantity`, `Actual Quantity`, `Planned Cost`, and `Actual Cost` as separate fields. | Plan-versus-actual analysis requires explicit separation of quantities and costs. |
| `BR-034` | Forecast timing is not final actual timing | Schedule | `Actual Duration` is a final value for completed rows, while in-progress projection must use `Forecast Start`, `Forecast Finish`, and `Remaining Duration`. | Prevents mixing completed performance with forward-looking projection logic. |
| `BR-035` | Status date is sheet-level | Schedule | `Status Date` must be stored once per schedule sheet version rather than repeated per row. | The pilot uses one data date per control surface. |
| `BR-036` | Item kind and item type are separate | Schedule | `Item Kind` stores the primary row kind, while `Item Type` stores optional subtype detail and is required for `event` rows. | Separates primary row meaning from extensible subtype detail. |
| `BR-037` | Responsible uses controlled responsibility buckets | Schedule | `Responsible` must be chosen from a controlled responsibility dictionary and must not be a person-name field. | Stable accountability grouping is needed for analytics and bot answers. |
| `BR-038` | WBS rows are structural only | Schedule | `wbs` rows are structural summary rows and must not carry direct dependency chains or operational actual logic. | Prevents confusion between hierarchy carriers and actionable schedule rows. |
| `BR-039` | Baseline may store planned progress as of status date | Schedule | `schedule_baseline` may store planned `Percent Complete` and derived `Status` for actionable non-`wbs` rows as of the sheet `Status Date`, while `Actual*` fields remain blank. | Supports planned-as-of-date analytics without confusing baseline with actual execution. |
| `BR-040` | WBS rows stay blank for pilot progress and cost | Schedule | In the pilot, `wbs` rows must remain blank for `Percent Complete`, `Status`, and `Planned Cost`. | Avoids premature WBS roll-up semantics and keeps structural rows lightweight. |
| `BR-041` | Planned cost follows the normative cost reference | Schedule | `Planned Cost` assignment must follow the normalized basis and allocation logic defined in `docs/business/dictionaries/planned_cost_reference.md`. | Prevents inconsistent pricing logic and double counting across delivery, procurement, and execution rows. |
| `BR-042` | Actual-field completion validation is current-only | Schedule | Validation rules that require `Actual Start` and `Actual Finish` on `done` rows apply to `schedule_current`, not to `schedule_baseline` planned statusing. | Baseline may be `done` in planned terms while intentionally keeping all `Actual*` fields blank. |
| `BR-043` | Status date is live in the workbook | Schedule | In the implemented pilot workbook, `Status Date` is maintained as `=TODAY()` on both `schedule_baseline` and `schedule_current`. | Keeps portfolio and status views aligned with the live control date without manual maintenance. |
| `BR-044` | Phase is a separate portfolio coordinate | Schedule | `Phase` is a manual dictionary-backed field that captures one of the 7 portfolio phases and does not replace `Stage`. | Portfolio filtering needs a stable macro-phase coordinate while `Stage` remains a lifecycle field inside a function. |
| `BR-045` | Portfolio baseline spans P01-P07 | Schedule | The active pilot `schedule_baseline` must contain cloned `P01-P07` projects so that all 7 macro phases are represented on the live control date. | The pilot needs one portfolio baseline surface that covers the full lifecycle spectrum in one workbook. |
